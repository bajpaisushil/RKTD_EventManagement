# Extras

## Dinner plates
A dinner plate costs $12 per person, per day. You can add dinner for any day that person is coming, including for under-5s. You can't add dinner on a day someone isn't booked to attend.

## Garba Workshop
A place in the Garba Workshop costs $20. The workshop runs only on Wednesday 11 November and Friday 13 November, and only a handful of places are left. Each place is for one person on one of those days, and that person must be coming that day.
Also asked: When is the dance class? Which days can I learn Garba?

## Dandiya sticks
A pair of souvenir dandiya sticks costs $5, collected at the festival. You can buy up to 20 pairs in one booking.

## Promo codes and extras
A code that takes money off the whole booking also reduces extras. A code that makes child tickets free only covers child Day and All-Day passes, not extras like a child's dinner plate.
Also asked: Do discounts apply to dinner and other extras?
