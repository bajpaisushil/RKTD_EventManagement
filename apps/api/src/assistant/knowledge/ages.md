# Ages

## Age rules
Children under 5 come in free and don't need a ticket. Children aged 5 to 11 need a child ticket. Everyone aged 12 and over needs an adult ticket.

## Which age counts
Ages are worked out on the first day of the festival, Tuesday 10 November 2026, and that age is used for the whole festival. A child who turns 12 on Thursday 12 November is still 11 on the first day, so they pay the child price on every day. A child who turns 5 during the festival comes in free all week.

## Why we ask for a date of birth
We ask for each person's date of birth rather than their age, because the festival lasts five days and "age" gets confusing when a birthday falls in the middle. The date of birth lets the booking system work out the right ticket.

## Under-5s
Under-5s are free, but please add them to your booking with their name and date of birth. That way the volunteers on the door know who to expect, and you can add a dinner plate for them if you'd like one.
Also asked: Is my toddler free? Do babies and young children need a ticket?

## The person booking
The person making the booking and paying must be 18 or over on the day they book. They don't have to come to the festival. A parent can book for teenagers or grandparents without buying a ticket for themselves.

## Proof of age
Child prices are for ages 5 to 11. Volunteers on the door may ask a child their age or date of birth, so please make sure the dates of birth in your booking are correct.

## Children with an adult
Children must be with an adult at the festival. If a booking has children on a day when nobody aged 18 or over in that booking is coming, the booking form shows a reminder. That's fine if their adult has a separate booking.
