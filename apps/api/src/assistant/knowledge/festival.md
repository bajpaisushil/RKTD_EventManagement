# Sharad Utsav 2026

## When and where
Sharad Utsav 2026 runs for five days, from Tuesday 10 November to Saturday 14 November 2026, at Radha Krishna Temple in Allen, Texas. All prices are in US dollars.

## What's on
The festival runs every day from Tuesday 10 to Saturday 14 November. Alongside entry, you can add dinner plates for any day you're coming, places in the Garba Workshop (Wednesday 11 and Friday 13 November only), and souvenir pairs of dandiya sticks.

## Things this guide doesn't cover
Daily start and end times, parking, food menus, accessibility arrangements and the cultural programme aren't covered here. For those, please contact the temple directly. The assistant won't guess them.

## What Sharad Utsav celebrates
Sharad means autumn. At Radha Krishna temples, autumn celebrations are traditionally linked with the Raas Leela, Krishna's circle dance with the gopis under the autumn full moon. That's why circle dances like Garba and Dandiya Raas sit at the heart of the festival.

## Garba and Dandiya Raas
Garba is a folk dance from Gujarat, danced in circles with clapping and graceful turns. Dandiya Raas is danced in pairs with decorated sticks, called dandiya, that dancers strike together in rhythm. Beginners are welcome, and the Garba Workshop is a good place to learn the steps.
