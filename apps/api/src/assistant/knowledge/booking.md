# Booking and payment

## How to book
Use the booking form on this page. First add your own details and everyone who's coming, with their dates of birth. Then pick tickets and days, add any extras, and check everything before paying. The total updates as you go and is always calculated by the booking system, so what you see is what you pay.

## Payment
Payment is by card through Stripe's secure checkout page. When you go to pay, your places are held for 30 minutes. If you don't finish paying in that time, the hold ends, nothing is charged and the places go back on sale. If you leave the payment page, your details stay in the form so you can try again.
Also asked: How long do you hold my places while I pay?

## Free bookings
If a promo code brings your total to $0, the booking is confirmed straight away without a payment step.

## Confirmation
Once you've paid, you'll see a confirmation page with your booking reference (it looks like SU-ABCD-1234) and a QR code to show at the door. We also email a copy to the address you gave. You can print the page or save it as a PDF.

## Promo codes
You can use one promo code per booking. Enter it on the last step, before paying. Codes don't care about capital letters or spaces. Some codes have an end date or a limited number of uses. The form tells you if a code has expired, has been used up, or doesn't apply to your booking. The assistant can't share or suggest codes.

## Changes, cancellations and refunds
Paid bookings can't be changed or cancelled online. Please contact the temple about changes or refunds. The assistant can't change bookings.
Also asked: Can I get a refund or my money back?

## Group size
One booking can include up to 20 people, under-5s included. For a bigger group, please make more than one booking.

## Booking during the festival
You don't need to book ahead. Each day stays on sale online until 11:59pm Central Time that day. At 12:00am, when the next day starts, it closes; to request a place on a day that has ended, email ravi_chandra@jkyog.org. All-Day Passes stop selling at 12:00am on Tuesday 10 November, when the festival starts, and sales close after Saturday 14 November.
