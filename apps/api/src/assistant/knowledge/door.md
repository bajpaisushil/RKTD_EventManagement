# At the festival

## Checking in at the door
Show your QR code or booking reference to the volunteers at the door. They can look up your booking by reference or by name and check each person in for that day. Each person can only be checked in on the days they're booked for.

## What volunteers see
Volunteers see each person's name, age, ticket, and whether they have dinner or the Garba Workshop that day. They also see how many pairs of dandiya sticks to hand over.

## Lost your reference
If you can't find your confirmation, a volunteer can search for your booking by your name.
Also asked: I can't find my confirmation email or booking reference.
