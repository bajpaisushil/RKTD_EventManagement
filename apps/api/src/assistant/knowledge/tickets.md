# Tickets and prices

## Ticket types
There are five kinds of entry ticket. Day Pass for adults (age 12 and over): $15 per person, per day, for any days you choose. Day Pass for children (age 5 to 11): $8 per person, per day. All-Day Pass for adults: $60, covering all five days. All-Day Pass for children: $30, covering all five days. Family Pass: $40 for one day, covering 2 adults and 2 children.
Also asked: How much does it cost to get in? How much is it for one day?

## One kind of ticket per person
Each person in a booking holds exactly one kind of ticket: a Day Pass for the days they pick, an All-Day Pass, or a place on a Family Pass. You don't choose between the adult and child version. The booking form works that out from each person's date of birth.

## All-Day Pass
An All-Day Pass covers all five festival days and can't be changed to fewer days. The adult All-Day Pass is sold out, so adults coming every day need a Day Pass for each day ($75 for all five). The child All-Day Pass ($30) is still available. All-Day Passes stop selling once the festival has started, because they would only cover the days that are left.
Also asked: Is there a pass for the whole festival, or for every night?

## When an All-Day Pass is cheaper
A child coming on four or five days pays $32 or $40 with Day Passes, so the $30 child All-Day Pass is cheaper. The booking form points this out and lets you switch with one tap.

## Family Pass
A Family Pass costs $40 and covers exactly 2 adults (age 12 and over) and 2 children (age 5 to 11) on one day. That's $6 less than buying their Day Passes separately. A family coming on several days buys one Family Pass for each day. If your group has more people, for example a third child, the extra people take their own Day Pass. Children under 5 come free alongside and don't take a place on the pass. A group of 2 adults and 1 child, or 1 adult and 2 children, doesn't fit a Family Pass, and separate Day Passes are cheaper for them anyway.

## Teenagers
Anyone aged 12 or over on the first day of the festival is priced as an adult. A 13-year-old counts as one of the adults on a Family Pass, not one of the children.

## Sold out and limited items
The number of tickets and extras left is checked live. Some items are limited: the adult All-Day Pass is sold out, and the Garba Workshop has very few places. Your places are only guaranteed once you've paid.
